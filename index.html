Long-term Vision

JARVIS / FRIDAY-like Personal AI

One of my long-term goals is to explore personal AI inspired by JARVIS / FRIDAY from Iron Man. I envision an assistant that can not only answer questions, use tools, and handle everyday tasks, but also gradually understand its user, build lasting memory, recognize context, offer help when appropriate, and continue learning and growing through long-term interaction and collaboration.

SAO-like AI-Native Worlds

Another long-term dream of mine is to help build highly immersive virtual worlds like the one portrayed in Sword Art Online (SAO).

The AI-native worlds I envision are more than vast environments with rich interactions. They are living, evolving worlds where characters are no longer scripted NPCs, but individuals capable of remembering experiences, forming relationships, making their own choices, and learning and growing alongside players, other characters, and the world itself — creating stories that continue to unfold over time.

I also remain interested in the long-term development of AGI. For now, I prefer to focus on concrete problems in agent memory, learning, planning, decision-making, and interaction, exploring how intelligent agents can accumulate experience, continue growing, and gradually become more autonomous and general-purpose.
